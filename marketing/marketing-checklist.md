# Seven-day launch plan

**Goal:** the first 10 genuine testers who install the APK, scan a code with a
real phone camera and tell you something back.

**Budget:** zero. No ads, no paid promotion, no scheduling tools, no
subscriptions, no bots. Everything below is manual, by you, in short sessions.

**Before day 1 (about an hour)**

- [ ] Confirm the release page, download link and SHA-256 in the notes.
- [ ] Read the rules of every community you plan to post in. Write down which
      ones allow developer showcase posts. Skip the rest.
- [ ] Decide how you sign your posts (name or handle) and where replies land
      (GitHub Issues). No invented email address.
- [ ] Capture three real screenshots from the installed APK for the README and
      any post that allows images.

---

## Day 1: base camp

- [ ] Enable GitHub Pages on the repository so the landing page is live.
- [ ] Check the landing page loads, the download link works and the disclaimer
      is visible.
- [ ] Post the Reddit draft in the single most relevant community that permits
      it. One post only.
- [ ] Note the post time. Reply to every comment for the next two hours.

## Day 2: issue hygiene

- [ ] Read every comment and issue. Answer questions, do not argue.
- [ ] Fix or document anything that blocks a first scan.
- [ ] Update the release notes if you found a wrong claim.

## Day 3: second channel

- [ ] Post the community draft in one other place (a Discord "showcase" channel,
      a forum thread, Mastodon or Bluesky). Again, one place.
- [ ] Ask one concrete question: "Does the Wi-Fi code join your network on the
      first scan?"

## Day 4: the video

- [ ] Record the 15-20 second demo from `short-video-script.md` on a real phone.
- [ ] Publish it with the release link in the description and the beta
      disclosure in the caption.
- [ ] No reposting the same clip to five platforms in one hour. One today, one
      tomorrow.

## Day 5: the Wi-Fi angle

- [ ] Send two or three outreach messages from `small-business-outreach.md`.
      Real businesses you know, hand written.
- [ ] Post in one community where Wi-Fi QR codes are genuinely on topic (hospitality
      or networking discussion, not a promo board).

## Day 6: collect

- [ ] Chase the testers who replied with thanks and one follow-up question.
- [ ] Log feedback in GitHub Issues: what broke, what confused people, what
      they assumed the app could do but cannot.
- [ ] Do not launch anywhere new today. Consolidate.

## Day 7: review

- [ ] Count installs you can actually evidence (release download count) against
      replies received. Ten downloads with zero replies is not ten testers.
- [ ] Write three lines to yourself: which channel produced a real tester, which
      produced nothing, what you will not repeat.
- [ ] Update `BETA.md`/release notes with anything testers taught you.

---

## Counting testers honestly

A tester is someone who installed the APK **and** reported back. Track:

| Who | Channel | Reported a scan result? |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

Do not inflate the number with download counts, and do not create accounts to
comment on your own posts.

## What not to do

- No spam, no mass messaging, no scraped contact lists.
- No fake reviews, no fabricated quotes, no purchased installs or downloads.
- No automatic posting or scheduling services.
- No reposting the same text across many communities on the same day.
- No claims that the app is unique, audited, completely secure or better than
  the alternatives. Say what it does and show it working.
