## Draft

I released the beta of QR Generator, a small Android app that makes QR codes
offline.

It handles text, URLs, email, phone numbers and Wi-Fi credentials. The Wi-Fi
form takes the network name, password, security type and a hidden-network flag,
so a scan can join without anyone retyping the password. Everything is
generated on the phone: no account, no server, no analytics, and it works in
airplane mode.

Android only for now. The APK is a GitHub Release, not a Play Store listing, so
you sideload it and Android asks you to allow installs from that source.

This beta ships with purchases disabled and premium features unlocked, so
testers get custom colors, rounded/dot styles, logo overlay, unlimited saves
and history at no cost.

Looking for: people who will scan a Wi-Fi code with a stock camera app and tell
me whether it joins, plus any crash reports.
