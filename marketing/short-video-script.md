# 15-20 second demo video script

**Status:** draft for your approval. Record on a real phone running the real
APK. Do not recreate the interface in a mockup and present it as the app.

**Format:** vertical (9:16), screen recording with your voice over, or a phone
filmed on a desk. No music needed. Total 15-20 seconds.

---

## Script

| Time | Visual | Voice over / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Phone in airplane mode, Wi-Fi settings open, camera off. Big text on screen: "Type the Wi-Fi password one more time." | Hook: "Stop typing the Wi-Fi password for guests." |
| 3-7s | Open QR Generator, tap **Wi-Fi**, enter SSID and password, tick hidden network if shown. QR updates live in the preview. | "This app turns your network into a QR code." |
| 7-11s | Tap Save PNG or Share, then switch to the stock camera app and point it at the code. | "Everything happens on the phone. No account, no internet." |
| 11-15s | Camera recognizes the code, shows the network, join prompt appears. | "Guests scan, they join. That's the whole app." |
| 15-18s | Show the app icon, then the release page on screen. | "Free beta, APK in my GitHub. Link below." |

**On-screen caption for the whole clip:** "Free beta, not on Google Play,
purchases disabled."

---

## Recording checklist

- Use the actual beta APK, dark or light theme, real Wi-Fi credentials of a
  network you control (never a real guest network password you care about).
- Airplane mode on for the whole take: it is the honest proof of offline
  behavior.
- Crop out notifications, your name, and the full password as you type it (type
  it blurred or use a test network like `Cafe-Guest-Test`).
- Film the QR fill at least 70% of the frame so viewers can see it is a real
  code.
- Do not claim speed, security or superiority. Show what happens.

## Publishing

- YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Mastodon/Bluesky video: same file, same caption.
- Title: "Type the Wi-Fi password one more time" or "Wi-Fi QR codes, offline".
- Description must include: beta APK link, "independent beta, not on Google
  Play", "purchases disabled, premium unlocked for testers", and that you
  developed the app.
