# Small business outreach message

**Status:** draft for your approval. Send manually, one business at a time, to
places you would actually walk into. This is a conversation opener, not a blast.

**Rules for using it**

- Send no more than two or three messages per day, to businesses you have a
  real reason to contact.
- Never buy a contact list, never use a scraped harvester, never automate
  sending. One human, one message, one business.
- If someone says no or does not reply, move on. Do not follow up more than
  once.
- Be reachable: sign the message with your name or handle and link the public
  repository so the business can inspect what it would be handing to guests.
- Do not claim the app is audited, unhackable or better than existing menu/QR
  services. Say what it does and let them decide.

---

## Message (email or contact form)

**Subject:** Free offline Wi-Fi QR code for your venue

Hello,

I am developing an Android app called QR Generator. One of the things it does
well is the case cafés and small shops hit every day: it turns your Wi-Fi
network name and password into a QR code that a guest scans with the phone
camera to join.

For your venue that means no more writing the password on the chalkboard or
spelling it out across the counter. The code is generated on the phone, it does
not need an internet connection, and there is no account or subscription.

I am looking for a handful of venues to test the beta. If you are interested I
would send you the install link and a short note on printing the code for your
counter or receipt. Feedback from real staff would genuinely shape what I fix
next.

Source code and the download are public here: [LINK_TO_REPO]

If it is not useful, tell me and I will not follow up.

Thanks for your time,

## After they reply

1. Send the release link and the sideload instructions from `BETA.md`.
2. Ask one specific question: did the code join the network on the first scan,
   and did staff find it obvious?
3. Ask permission before quoting their feedback anywhere.
